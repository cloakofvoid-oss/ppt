01 · The Organization

What exactly
is IEEE?

IEEE — formerly known as the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers — is a global organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

It's the world's largest technical professional organization, with a core mission to foster technological innovation and excellence across a wide range of disciplines.

02 · Your Chapter

The IEEE
Student Branch

A local community of student members within a university or college, chartered by IEEE. It serves as a hub for students interested in technology, offering a platform to connect with peers, faculty, and industry professionals.

The primary goal of an IEEE Student Branch is to enhance students' educational, technical, and professional development.

06 · Conferences

Where engineers
show their work.

IEEE organizes over 2,000 conferences every year across the globe — where the world's engineers and researchers present work, exchange ideas, and spark innovation.

It's not just for professors or PhD scholars. Students can attend workshops, present a paper, and network with industry experts. Presenting at an IEEE conference and getting published in the official proceedings is a genuine career milestone.

08 · Tech4Good & HTB

Technology for
real community impact.

IEEE Tech4Good is part of the IEEE Humanitarian Technologies Board (HTB) and supports grassroots technology projects aimed at addressing local community challenges, aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

HTB — formerly the Humanitarian Activities Committee — empowers IEEE volunteers to design and deliver impactful, ethical humanitarian technology initiatives addressing societal challenges globally.

09 · EPICS in IEEE

Engineering Projects
in Community Service.

A global program where students, academic mentors, and nonprofit or community partners team up to design technology-based solutions for real-world problems.

The focus is sustainable impact, not just an academic exercise — you solve issues faced by local communities while learning engineering and project-management skills.
